About DailyKit

DailyKit works fully offline. All your data — names, dates, and notes — stays stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to a server, so there is no account, login, or cloud sync involved.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I lose my data if I reinstall the app?

Since everything is stored only on your device, uninstalling or reinstalling the app can erase your data. Back up important information before doing so, if the app offers an export option.

Can I use DailyKit on multiple devices?

Because there's no account or cloud sync, data does not automatically transfer between devices.

The app is not working as expected. What should I do?

Try restarting the app first. If the issue continues, reach out to us using the contact option below with details of the problem and your device model.

Is my data ever collected or shared?

No. DailyKit does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, since it never connects to a server.
